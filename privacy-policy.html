Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 01, 2026

01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used and protected when you visit www.vaibhavbagaria.com or use any of the services offered through it.

This website is operated on behalf of Dr. Vaibhav Bagaria, Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon, practising at Sir H. N. Reliance Foundation Hospital, Girgaon, Mumbai. References to "we", "us" and "our" mean the practice of Dr. Vaibhav Bagaria.

By using this website, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use this website or submit information through it.

02 Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Contact and appointment details such as your name, telephone number, email address, age and city.
  • Clinical information you choose to share, including a description of your symptoms, your medical history, and current medication.
  • Medical images and reports you upload, including X-rays, MRI and CT scans and their accompanying reports.
  • Correspondence you send us by email, contact form or WhatsApp.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Browser type, device type and operating system.
  • Pages viewed, time spent on the site and referring website.
  • Cookie and analytics data.

We do not collect payment card details through this website. Any payment for consultation or treatment is handled directly by Sir H. N. Reliance Foundation Hospital under its own policies.

03 Health information

Medical images, reports and clinical details you share with us are treated as sensitive personal data under Indian law.

  • They are collected only where you choose to provide them, and only for the purpose of clinical assessment or answering your enquiry.
  • They are accessible only to Dr. Bagaria and authorised members of his clinical and administrative team.
  • They are not sold, rented or shared for marketing purposes at any time.
  • They are not used in any publication, presentation or promotional material without your separate written consent.

Important: Please do not upload medical records belonging to another person unless you are authorised to do so on their behalf.

04 How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry or request.
  • To arrange, confirm or reschedule appointments.
  • To provide an opinion on scans or reports you have submitted.
  • To maintain clinical records where you become a patient.
  • To improve the content and performance of this website.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory and medical record-keeping obligations.

An opinion given in response to a scan or enquiry submitted through this website is preliminary and does not constitute a diagnosis or a treatment plan. It does not replace an in-person consultation.

05 Consent

Information is collected on the basis of your consent, which you give by submitting it through this website.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us at [EMAIL]. Where you withdraw consent, we will stop processing your information for the purposes described above, subject to any records we are legally required to retain.

06 Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Information may be shared only:

  • With Sir H. N. Reliance Foundation Hospital where necessary for your appointment, admission or treatment.
  • With other healthcare professionals involved in your care, where clinically appropriate.
  • With service providers who support the operation of this website, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Where required by law, court order, or a regulatory or government authority.
  • Where necessary to protect the safety of a person.

07 Third-party services

This website uses third-party services which may collect data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager for website analytics.
  • Google Maps for location display.
  • YouTube for embedded video.
  • WhatsApp where you choose to contact us through it.

We do not control how these providers process data collected through their platforms. Please refer to their respective privacy policies.

08 WhatsApp communication

If you contact us via WhatsApp, your message and phone number are received on a device used by the practice. WhatsApp is a third-party service and messages are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and security arrangements.

Please do not send sensitive medical records or detailed clinical history over WhatsApp. Use the secure upload facility on this website or bring your records to your consultation.

09 Cookies

This website uses cookies to enable core functionality, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how parts of this website function.

10 Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information submitted through this website, including encrypted transmission and restricted access to uploaded files.

No method of transmission over the internet is entirely secure. While we take care to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to this website.

11 Data retention

  • Enquiry and appointment information is retained for as long as necessary to respond to you and for a reasonable period afterwards.
  • Clinical records for patients are retained in accordance with applicable medical record-keeping requirements.
  • Uploaded scans from enquiries that do not result in a consultation are deleted after a reasonable period.
  • Analytics data is retained according to the settings of the analytics provider.

12 Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Request erasure of your information, subject to records we are legally required to retain.
  • Withdraw consent to further processing.
  • Raise a grievance about how your information has been handled.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@drvaibhavbagaria.com.

13 Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian. Where a minor is a patient, information is provided and consented to by a parent or legal guardian.

14 Links to other websites

This website contains links to external sites, including hospital pages, medical directories, research profiles and media coverage. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites.

15 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the website after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
